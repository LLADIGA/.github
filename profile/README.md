**LLADIGA** (*Learning Language with Dialogue Games*) is a 3-year bilateral research project investigating whether artificial agents can learn language more efficiently—and less ecologically damagingly—by learning to use language **for a purpose** through multi-turn, goal-oriented dialogue games.

- **Consortium:** [University of Potsdam](https://www.uni-potsdam.de/en/) & [Free University of Bozen-Bolzano (unibz)](https://www.unibz.it/)
- **Principal Investigators:** [Prof. Dr. David Schlangen](https://www.ling.uni-potsdam.de/~das/) (Potsdam) & [Prof. Raffaella Bernardi](https://www.unibz.it/en/faculties/engineering/academic-staff/person/2311-raffaella-bernardi) (unibz)
- **Organized Workshop & Shared Task:** [The LM Playschool @ EMNLP 2026](https://lm-playschool.github.io/) ([ACL Anthology Proceedings](https://aclanthology.org/2026.lmplayschool-1.9/))
- **Funding:** Bilateral collaboration framework between the **Deutsche Forschungsgemeinschaft (DFG)** (SPP 2556 *LaSTing*) and the **Autonomous Province of Bolzano – South Tyrol** (*D-SUD LAV*).
